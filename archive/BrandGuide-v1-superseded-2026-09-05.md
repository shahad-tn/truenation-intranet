# Community Brand Guide — FIRST EDITION (SUPERSEDED)

**Status: SUPERSEDED 2026-09-05. Do not follow this document.**

Superseded by the second edition, which corrects three rules that produced
text nobody with low vision could read. Kept here as the record of what was
specified and when, so the reasons for the change stay traceable.

## Why it was replaced

| First edition said | Measured | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Gold `#C9972C` may be used as text on Warm Snow, "4.6:1" | **2.49:1** | 65 instances of unreadable gold text shipped across the intranet |
| Control borders `#86868F` | **2.95:1** | Fails WCAG 1.4.11 by 0.05 — control edges not perceivable |
| Warm Snow `#FAF8F4` as page ground | — | Retired 2026-08-29; stacked warm neutrals gave ~2% surface separation and caused eye fatigue |
| "Never use any colour outside the four-colour palette" | — | Left forms unable to display an error state |
| Squarespace colour map | — | The site runs on Vercel + Google Apps Script |

The guide contained no contrast requirements, no focus-indicator guidance, and
no status colours. Its colour psychology, typography rationale, type hierarchy,
brand voice and logo rules were sound and carry forward unchanged.

Note that the source PDF was never in this repo — it existed only as a Claude
Project attachment and in the Drive brand assets folder. What follows is its
extracted text.

---

# TRUE NATION
AWAKEN · RESTORE · UNITE
COMMUNITY BRAND GUIDE
Colors · Typography · Usage · Do's & Don'ts · Claude Prompt

COMMUNITY SERVED
Black, Latino, and Native American (BIPOC) communities and Congregants.

VISION
To awaken, restore, and unite Black, Latino, and Native American communities by
teaching their true identity as the Israelites according to Scripture.

MISSION
We use Scripture-based education to heal trauma, reconnect our people to
purpose, and guide them toward righteousness through private classes, youth
training, and spiritual development.

CORE VALUES
Excellence · Accountability · Faith · Holiness · Order · Perseverance

## COLOR PALETTE (as originally specified)

- `#7C1316` Dark Wine — PRIMARY. Anchors the brand in authority, sacrifice and
  spiritual depth. Reflects the blood covenant at the core of the mission.
  Embodies Holiness, Perseverance and Order.
- `#C9972C` Sovereign Gold — SECONDARY, UI ACCENTS ONLY. Divine favour,
  excellence, refinement. Amber-toned, earned not flashy. Echoes the Temple,
  Menorah and Ark. **"Gold text allowed on light backgrounds only" — THIS RULE
  WAS WRONG, it measures 2.49:1.**
- `#FAF8F4` Warm Snow — BACKGROUND. "No pure white is used."
- `#130D0A` Deep Brown-Black — BODY TEXT on light.
- `#F2EDE4` Warm Cream — all text on Burgundy/dark.

## TYPOGRAPHY (carried forward unchanged)

Header — Barlow Condensed Bold, uppercase. "Condensed fonts signal strength
under pressure — power without excess. A visual metaphor for True Nation:
people who have endured and still stand tall."

Body — DM Sans Regular/Medium/Bold. "Humanist and warm — drawn to feel closer
to handwriting than machinery. Where Barlow proclaims, DM Sans listens."

| Role | Font | Size | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 Page Title | Barlow Condensed Bold | 36–48px | Uppercase |
| H2 Section | Barlow Condensed Bold | 24–32px | Uppercase |
| H3 Card/Label | Barlow Condensed SemiBold | 18–22px | Uppercase |
| Body | DM Sans Regular | 14–16px | Sentence case |
| Caption/Tag | DM Sans Medium | 11–12px | Uppercase + letter spacing |
| Button/CTA | DM Sans Bold | 13–14px | Uppercase |

## BRAND VOICE (carried forward unchanged)

DO: write with spiritual authority and grounding; reference Scripture when
appropriate; lead with purpose and mission; use "our people" and "our
community".

DON'T: casual slang in official copy; dilute or sidestep the Scriptural
mission; imagery that undermines dignity; speak *for* the community rather
than *with* it.

## LOGO (carried forward unchanged)

Use the official TN Community logo from Drive. Never stretch, rotate, distort,
recreate or redraw it. Never place on busy or photographic backgrounds. Never
use unauthorised colour versions. Maintain clear space at all times.
