Rename "Stewardship" to "Safety & Facilities"?
In churches, "Stewardship" almost universally means financial/resource stewardship — it will be confused with the Finance department. ERT + Security is more accurately a safety/facilities function. Options:
A. Safety & Facilities — most descriptive of what ERT + Security actually do
B. Campus Safety — clear, campus-context language
C. Keep "Stewardship" — if you're expanding it to include Maintenance + Custodial as well (stewardship of physical spaces)
2
Is Shawashan's "Logistics" IT-work or operations-work?
If Shawashan handles physical logistics — event setup, equipment transport, coordination — that belongs under Clerical/Administration, not IT. IT logistics implies hardware procurement, device deployment, etc. Which is it?
A. Move to Clerical — operational logistics (events, transport, coordination)
B. Keep in IT — IT-specific logistics (hardware, tech deployment)
3
PARADOX App — Production or IT/Applications?
There's a difference between PARADOX (the media content/show — lives in Production) and PARADOX App (the software application — arguably lives in IT/Applications). Should these be split?
A. Split them — PARADOX show under Production, PARADOX App under IT/Applications
B. Keep both under Production — they're the same project, Shahad just happens to build the app

Who leads Finance?
Finance has active sub-group leads (Marayam, Ahhabyah, Shabayah, Banayah) but the department lead is TBD. Is there someone currently acting in that role, or is it genuinely an open seat?
