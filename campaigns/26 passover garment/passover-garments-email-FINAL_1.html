Shalom,
Garments for the men are $175 each. Rather than ask anyone to hand that over in one shot, we set it up as monthly payments that come out on their own.
That's what we're recommending — set up a monthly auto-payment. Zeffy pulls the same amount on the same day every month until your garment is paid off. You handle it once and then it's off your plate. No reminders to chase, nothing to remember, no scrambling in November.
One thing to know: Zeffy charges you on the same date you sign up. Sign up on the 10th, it pulls on the 10th every month. So the sooner you set it up, the smaller each payment is.

Setting Up Your Page
One time, about two minutes.
1
Open the link
The Become a fundraiser window opens. Enter your info there.
Set Up My Page
2
Fill it out, then click Create campaign
Solo campaign is already selected — it's the only option, so leave it alone. Feel free to add a photo.
Campaign target $175 × how many garments you're buying. One garment $175, two $350, three $525, four $700.
First / last name Yours — this is how we know whose page it is
Email Where your receipts go
3
Bookmark this page, then make your first payment
Your campaign is live. Click View campaign and bookmark it. This is your page — open it again any time to pay or to check where you stand.
Then pick your monthly amount from the presets, or type your own if you're buying more than one garment.
4
Answer the questions and donate
Full Name(s) of Garment is required — list everyone the garments are being made for, yourself included.
You'll also see an optional contribution to Zeffy. Zeffy is free for the congregation because they ask donors for a tip. It's optional. To skip it, set the dropdown to Other and enter $0 — your total will be exactly what you meant to pay.
 
One Last Thing — Stopping Your Payments
Monthly payments keep going until somebody stops them. They will not stop on their own once you hit $175.
So after your final payment, cancel it from your Zeffy account — or email support@truenation.org and Finance will stop it for you. Either way works. Just don't skip it.
 
If the setup gives you any trouble at all, email support@truenation.org and someone will walk you through it. Getting stuck is not a reason to fall behind.
We know this takes coordination on your end, and we don't take that lightly. Thawadah for your cooperation and your consideration.
