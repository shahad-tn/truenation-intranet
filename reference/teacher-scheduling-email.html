Shalom, teachers,

We now have one portal for scheduling both weekly classes. It knows who you are when you sign in with your @truenation.org account, so there's no separate login. Here's how each class works.

Open the Teacher Portal →
You must be a member of the Moreh (Teachers) group to enter.
Bible Basics — Tuesdays
You choose your topics

Bible Basics is first come, first served. Open the Bible Basics tab to:

1.Pick any open topic from the bucket and tap Claim.
2.Assign it to one of your Tuesdays. Your rotation dates are shown first; you may also sub in on any open Tuesday.
3.Claim as many topics as you like. Changed your mind? Release it back to the bucket.

Once a topic or a date is taken, it's locked to that teacher — so claim early. When all topics have been taught, the cycle resets and begins again.

World History — Wednesdays
Assigned by rotation

World History runs on a set rotation. Topics are taught in order, one each Wednesday, and each Wednesday has an assigned teacher — there's no topic to pick.

Open the World History tab to see your assigned Wednesdays and the exact topic for each. Need to cover a week or teach an extra one? Tap Teach this date to step in, and Give back to hand it back. Substituting never changes the standing rotation or the topic order.

Tip: the My Teaching tab gathers every upcoming date assigned to you across both classes in one place.

Covering for each other: on either class, if someone can't make their date, any teacher can tap Teach this date to step in. The standing rotation never changes — you're just covering that one session.

Questions or trouble signing in? Reach us at it@truenation.org.
