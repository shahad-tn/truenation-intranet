General Protocols

We as congregants strive toward excellency in all that we do and to work smarter not harder. General protocols are placed to keep our organization flowing in an organized manner and to make the experience for all members comfortable and pleasant. Here, we all contribute and are held to the same standards to keep our congregation orderly and unified (Colossians 4:5, Hebrews 13:16, Romans 13:3, Matthew 18:15-17).

  • If anything within the congregation needs improvement or a change, please bring it to the attention of one of the leaders. We want to create and maintain a culture of approachability, openness, respect, and excellency.
  • Guests may be orientated after 40 days of consistent attendance via in class or online.
  • Be mindful of how you conduct yourself on a day-to-day basis including what you post via social media. As we represent not only ourselves, but our households, organization, and the Most High.
  • Alms is $150/mo. per adult congregant.  (Numbers 1:3, 20)
  • Remember the Sabbaths and Feasts Days are holy convocations and as such are mandatory. In the event a brother or sister is not in attendance for 40 days or more AND has not communicated, you will be removed as a member of True Nation and will be reclassified as a guest/visitor and treated accordingly. If there is a situation in which you will not be an active congregant for a period of time, please communicate with the Bishops, Deacons or Apostles so all things can be done decently and in order. (Hebrews 10:25, Leviticus 23:1-2, Zephaniah 2:1, Psalms 95:2, Nehemiah 8:1-3)
  • For those who attend classes and are not dressed in righteous attire according to the scriptures, you will be asked to leave and allowed to return upon compliance to the law concerning holy and righteous attire. (Numbers 15:38-40, 1 Timothy 2:9, Deuteronomy 22:5, Zechariah 1:8, Judith 9:2, 2 Samuel 10:3-5)
  • Righteousness is an essential part of building the nation, and we at True Nation strive for brotherly and sisterly love exercised through the spirit of meekness, humility and correction. No one is above the law and as such we must all strive for growth mentally and spiritually. If a brother or sister is walking disorderly and the situation cannot be rectified in the mouth of two or three witnesses, then a formal counsel will be called to rectify the situation. In the event a judgement in the form of a suspension is deemed necessary here are the three levels of suspension referenced.
Level 1 — Can attend class just no extracurricular activities.
Level 2 — Removed temporarily from the body.
Level 3 — Removed from body permanently or indefinitely.

Respect the School

As a member, you are now a part of the body of this congregation. With that being said, here are ways all of us contribute to the upkeep of the school, support each other and hold each other accountable (2 Corinthians 8:13, 1 Corinthians 14:40, Philippians 2:2):

  • Help keep the school clean by practicing good housekeeping.
  • If you drop something, pick it up. If you spill something, clean it up. If you break something, fix it or inquire as to how it may be fixed or the cost to repair or replace it.
  • If a leader, ask you to do something please respectfully listen and if there is a problem with what you are asked to do please give the understanding as to why you can't do what is being asked.
  • If you see someone else do any of the above-mentioned things and they do not move accordingly, please respectfully bring it to their attention. If there is a problem in doing so, then grab 2-3 witnesses.
  • Please support our works through liking, sharing, and subscribing to what we do via social media.

What We Believe According to The Bible

Our doctrine rests on the plain reading of Scripture. Each belief below is set beside the passages that establish it — read them for yourself.

01

Salvation is only for Israel

The Messiah was sent to redeem his own people — the house of Israel — and to grant them repentance and the forgiveness of sins.

Matthew 15:24
But he answered and said, I am not sent but unto the lost sheep of the house of Israel.
Matthew 1:21
And she shall bring forth a son, and thou shalt call his name JESUS: for he shall save his people from their sins.
Luke 1:68
Blessed be the Lord God of Israel; for he hath visited and redeemed his people,
John 4:22
Ye worship ye know not what: we know what we worship: for salvation is of the Jews.
Acts 5:30
The God of our fathers raised up Jesus, whom ye slew and hanged on a tree.
Acts 5:31
Him hath God exalted with his right hand to be a Prince and a Saviour, for to give repentance to Israel, and forgiveness of sins.
02

Blacks, Hispanics & Natives are the Israelites in the Americas

The curses foretold in the law fell upon Israel and their seed as a sign — curses our people have borne, and others have not.

Deuteronomy 28:15
But it shall come to pass, if thou wilt not hearken unto the voice of the LORD thy God, to observe to do all his commandments and his statutes which I command thee this day; that all these curses shall come upon thee, and overtake thee:
What curses do Blacks / Hispanics experience that White people have not?
Deuteronomy 28:36-37
The LORD shall bring thee, and thy king which thou shalt set over thee, unto a nation which neither thou nor thy fathers have known; and there shalt thou serve other gods, wood and stone.
And thou shalt become an astonishment, a proverb, and a byword, among all the nations whither the LORD shall lead thee.
Deuteronomy 28:46
And they shall be upon thee for a sign and for a wonder, and upon thy seed for ever.
Deuteronomy 28:48
Therefore shalt thou serve thine enemies which the LORD shall send against thee, in hunger, and in thirst, and in nakedness, and in want of all things: and he shall put a yoke of iron upon thy neck, until he have destroyed thee.
Deuteronomy 28:68
And the LORD shall bring thee into Egypt again with ships, by the way whereof I spake unto thee, Thou shalt see it no more again: and there ye shall be sold unto your enemies for bondmen and bondwomen, and no man shall buy you.
Egypt represents bondage — the book of Exodus.
Job 9:24
The earth is given into the hand of the wicked: he covereth the faces of the judges thereof; if not, where, and who is he?
The wicked here points to the border of wickedness — Edom.
Israelis (whites in the land now) are not the Israelites
Brown brass — a figure of Christ.

Christ sprang out of Judah.

The Israelites are black / brown.
Proverbs 29:2

When the saints are back in the land, the world will rejoice.
03

The 7th-Day Sabbath — Sundown to Sundown

Scripture counts the day from evening to evening: the Sabbath begins when the sun goes down.

Judges 14:12
And Samson said unto them, I will now put forth a riddle unto you: if ye can certainly declare it me within the seven days of the feast, and find it out, then I will give you thirty sheets and thirty change of garments:
Judges 14:18
And the men of the city said unto him on the seventh day before the sun went down, What is sweeter than honey? and what is stronger than a lion? And he said unto them, If ye had not plowed with my heifer, ye had not found out my riddle.
Mark 15:42 KJV
And now when the even was come, because it was the preparation, that is, the day before the sabbath,
Mark 15:42 NLT
This all happened on Friday, the day of preparation, the day before the Sabbath. As evening approached,
A New Day Begins at Sundown
John 20:1
The first day of the week cometh Mary Magdalene early, when it was yet dark, unto the sepulchre, and seeth the stone taken away from the sepulchre.
John 20:19
Then the same day at evening, being the first day of the week, when the doors were shut where the disciples were assembled for fear of the Jews, came Jesus and stood in the midst, and saith unto them, Peace be unto you.
A new day begins at sundown — and note it was the doors of the house they hid in, not the gates of Jerusalem.
Nehemiah 13:19 KJV
And it came to pass, that when the gates of Jerusalem began to be dark before the sabbath, I commanded that the gates should be shut, and charged that they should not be opened till after the sabbath: and some of my servants set I at the gates, that there should no burden be brought in on the sabbath day.
The gates began to be dark before the sabbath — that is, at evening.

So I gave orders for the city gates to be shut at the beginning of every Sabbath, as soon as evening began to fall, and not to be opened again until the Sabbath was over. I stationed some of my men at the gates to make sure that nothing was brought into the city on the Sabbath.
At the beginning of every Sabbath, as soon as evening began to fall — going into night.
04

The New Moon Sabbath — the Crescent Moon

The New Moon is a holy convocation, kept from one new moon to another as Scripture and history testify.

Amos 8:5
Saying, When will the new moon be gone, that we may sell corn? and the sabbath, that we may set forth wheat, making the ephah small, and the shekel great, and falsifying the balances by deceit?
A Holy Convocation
Isaiah 66:23
And it shall come to pass, that from one new moon to another, and from one sabbath to another, shall all flesh come to worship before me, saith the LORD.
Historical Witness — The Sanhedrin

The proclamation of the New Moon — Ḳiddush ha-Ḥodesh — by the president of the Sanhedrin was of the greatest significance. Jewish Encyclopedia →

Historical Witness — Philo of Alexandria

Philo (c. 20 BCE – 50 CE), a Jewish philosopher writing before the Messiah, tied the festival cycle to the moon's phases: the moon waxes to the full at the end of the second week after its conjunction with the sun, marking the full moon of the lunar month.

05

Circumcision

Circumcision matters — you must be circumcised — but it profits only alongside repentance and the keeping of the law. If you are not living as Christ, you do not believe in Christ, and you should still be sacrificing.

Galatians 6:15
For in Christ Jesus neither circumcision availeth any thing, nor uncircumcision, but a new creature.
A new creature signifies repentance.
1 Corinthians 7:19
Circumcision is nothing, and uncircumcision is nothing, but the keeping of the commandments of God.
Romans 2:25
For circumcision verily profiteth, if thou keep the law: but if thou be a breaker of the law, thy circumcision is made uncircumcision.
John 14:15
If ye love me, keep my commandments.
Acts 7:8
And he gave him the covenant of circumcision: and so Abraham begat Isaac, and circumcised him the eighth day; and Isaac begat Jacob; and Jacob begat the twelve patriarchs.
Leviticus 12:1–3
And the LORD spake unto Moses, saying, Speak unto the children of Israel, saying, If a woman have conceived seed, and born a man child: then she shall be unclean seven days; according to the days of the separation for her infirmity shall she be unclean. And in the eighth day the flesh of his foreskin shall be circumcised.
